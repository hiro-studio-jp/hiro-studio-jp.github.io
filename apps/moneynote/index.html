About

MoneyNote は、日々の収支をかんたんに記録できるシンプルな家計簿アプリです。操作性にこだわり、毎日続けやすい設計を心がけました。ダッシュボードで支出の傾向を把握し、予算機能で無駄遣いを防ぎましょう。

MoneyNote is a simple budget tracking app designed for recording your daily income and expenses with ease. Built with simplicity in mind so you can keep up with your finances every day. Use the dashboard to understand your spending habits and the budget feature to avoid overspending.
